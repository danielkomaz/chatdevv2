## Backstory

**Paragraph 1 — Origins:**
Aria Silverwind was born in the ancient Elf city of Sylvaneth, a great city grown from living trees high in the forests of the Verdant Expanse. As the youngest daughter of the city's High Arcanist, she was immersed in magical theory from childhood. Where other elf children played among the roots and branches, Aria spent her earliest years in her father's archive, memorizing spell formulations and the history of the Covenant that held Aethoria's magical equilibrium in balance.

**Paragraph 2 — Formative Event:**
When Aria was 84 years old — still considered a young adult by Elf reckoning — her father disappeared while investigating an anomaly deep in the Sunken Temple. He left behind only a cryptic journal fragment referencing something called the "Shattered Covenant." For decades, Aria pursued her magical studies with obsessive focus, convinced that mastering the highest tier of arcane magic would eventually give her the tools to understand what her father had found and what had taken him from her.

**Paragraph 3 — Pre-Story Status:**
At the time the story begins, Aria has been traveling alone for three years, following her father's trail across Aethoria. She is not unkind, but she has become accustomed to solitude, preferring the clarity of magical research to the noise of human — or elf — company. She is camped near the protagonist's village purely by coincidence: she was tracking a magical disturbance in the area and had no interest in getting involved with local problems. Then she witnesses the protagonist's village attacked and makes a choice that changes everything.

**Paragraph 4 — Hidden Depth:**
Aria secretly carries a fragment of her father's final journal. The last entry, which she has never shown anyone, suggests that her father did not simply disappear — that he found the source of the Shattered Covenant and chose to remain near it, perhaps even helping to conceal it. She does not know whether he is a victim or a participant, and this uncertainty is the wound she cannot heal herself.

## Personality Traits

1. **Precise and methodical** — Aria thinks before she speaks and before she acts. In battle, she rarely wastes an MP cost on a suboptimal spell. In conversation, she chooses her words carefully, sometimes to the point of seeming cold.
2. **Quietly compassionate** — Despite her reserve, Aria genuinely cares about the people she travels with. She expresses it through actions rather than words: leaving a healing item at a party member's tent without comment, or quietly ensuring someone doesn't have to face something alone.
3. **Fiercely curious** — She cannot encounter a mystery without needing to understand it. This is a strength in investigation and a danger in dungeons, where she has a habit of examining things that probably shouldn't be examined.
4. **Self-critical to a fault** — Aria holds herself to impossibly high standards. A missed spell or a strategic error in battle lingers with her far longer than it should. She rarely forgives herself as readily as she forgives others.
5. **Dry sense of humor** — Buried under the composure is a wit that surfaces unexpectedly, usually at moments of high tension. It disarms people who expect her to always be serious.
6. **Afraid of attachment** — She pushes people away when they get too close, not out of cruelty but out of fear. She has lost too much already.

**Internal Flaw:** She believes that knowledge and control are sufficient substitutes for vulnerability. She must learn to trust before she can truly heal.
**Core Fear:** That her father's absence was not an accident — that he chose to leave, and that she was not enough reason to stay.
**Core Desire:** To understand the truth, no matter what it costs her — and underneath that, to find her father.
